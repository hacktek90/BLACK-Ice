Match in brief

Bangladesh’s pace sensation Nahid Rana produced a maiden ODI five-wicket haul to rip Pakistan out for 114, before Tanzid Hasan’s blistering 67* off 42 balls sealed an eight-wicket win with 209 balls to spare in the series opener at Mirpur. Opting to field, Bangladesh shot out the visitors in 30.4 overs; Rana finished with 5/24 and Mehidy Hasan Miraz claimed 3/29. In response, Tanzania Hasan and Najmul Hossain Shanto added 82 for the second wicket, and Bangladesh reached 115/2 in 15.1 overs to take a 1–0 lead in the three-match series.【turn2click0】【turn6click0】
