📚 How to Maintain Time

Use the Pomodoro Technique to maximize productivity.

  1. Set a Goal: Enter your task in the input field above.
  2. 25 Minutes Focus: Press Start. Work on only this task.
  3. Don't Leave: The AI will pause the timer if you look away or leave your desk. This forces you to be accountable.
  4. Short Break (5m): When the timer rings, take a 5-minute break. Stretch or grab water.
  5. Long Break (15m): After 4 completed sessions (dots), take a longer break.
⚠️ Important:
Keep this browser window visible on your screen. If you minimize the window, the camera stops working and the timer will drift.
