How this tool works

1. Upload: Use the background site to upload your file.

2. Share: Copy the file link, paste it in the bar, then tap to create a QR code.

3. Receive: Tap to scan someone else’s QR and open their file link.

Note: Camera access needs HTTPS or localhost. If it fails, check your browser permissions.
